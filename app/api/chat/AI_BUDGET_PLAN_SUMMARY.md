## Budget Monitoring

### Review Schedule

- **Monthly:** Actual vs. budget comparison, variance analysis
- **Quarterly:** Budget reforecast, ROI assessment
- **Annual:** Full budget review, next year planning

### Variance Thresholds

- **< 5%:** No action (normal variance)
- **5-10%:** Review and document
- **10-15%:** Manager review
- **15-25%:** Director review
- **> 25%:** Executive review

## Key Assumptions

1. **Conversation Volume:** 1,000/month base, 5% monthly growth
2. **Conversion Rate:** 15% stable
3. **Personnel:** No major turnover, 5% annual salary increases
4. **Infrastructure:** Current setup sufficient, predictable scaling

## Risk Factors

**High Impact:**
- Market competition (affects conversion rate)
- Economic conditions (affects deal value)
- Key personnel turnover (affects operations)
- Infrastructure failures (affects availability)

**Mitigation:**
- Regular market monitoring
- Diversified revenue streams
- Knowledge documentation
- Infrastructure redundancy
