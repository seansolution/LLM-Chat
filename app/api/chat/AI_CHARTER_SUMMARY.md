# AI Charter - Executive Summary

**Summary of the highest governing document for AI systems.**

---

## Overview

The AI Charter is the highest governing document for all AI systems operated by บริษัท แสน โซลูชั่น จำกัด (สำนักงานใหญ่). It establishes foundational principles, governance structure, and ethical framework for AI operations.

**Status:** Active  
**Authority:** Board of Directors and Executive Team  
**Supremacy:** Supersedes all other AI-related documents

---

## Key Components

### 1. Purpose (Article I)

**Mission:**
- Establish governance structure
- Ensure safety and compliance
- Promote ethics and transparency
- Enable business value
- Provide accountability

**Scope:** All AI systems, roles, interactions, and personnel

---

### 2. Scope (Article II)

**AI System Boundaries:**
- Permitted: Service/pricing questions, lead qualification, general support
- Prohibited: Legal advice, step-by-step procedures, price negotiation, out-of-scope operations

**Knowledge Sources:**
- Approved markdown files only
- Version-controlled knowledge base
- No unauthorized sources

---

### 3. Authority (Article III)

**Governance Structure:**
- **Board of Directors:** Ultimate authority, strategic oversight
- **Executive Team:** Strategic direction, resource allocation
- **AI Governance Board:** Day-to-day governance, policy enforcement
- **AI Operations Team:** System operations, development, maintenance

**Decision-Making Authority:**
- Kill Criteria: CTO, Data Protection Officer, Legal & Compliance, DevOps
- Exit Criteria: Executive Team, Risk Management Committee
- Policy Changes: AI Governance Board (minor), Executive Team (major), Board (Charter)

---

### 4. Governance Principles (Article IV)

**Core Principles:**
1. Safety First
2. Transparency
3. Human Oversight
4. Scope Control
5. Continuous Improvement
6. Compliance
7. Accountability

**Ethical Principles:**
1. Beneficence
2. Non-Maleficence
3. Autonomy
4. Justice
5. Transparency
6. Privacy

---

### 5. Ethics (Article V)

**Ethical Standards:**
- Respect human dignity
- Maintain honesty
- Protect privacy
- Ensure fairness
- Maintain transparency
- Prevent harm

**Forbidden Behaviors:**
- Legal/tax/accounting advice
- Step-by-step procedures
- Price negotiation
- Invented information
- Privacy violations
- Discrimination

**Enforcement:** Immediate shutdown for violations

---

### 6. Performance Review (Article VI)

**Review Framework:**
- Monthly: Performance, quality, incidents
- Quarterly: Strategic, financial, risk
- Annual: Comprehensive assessment, Charter review

**Key KPIs:**
- **Sales:** Conversion ≥ 15%, Revenue [Target], Cost/Conversion ≤ 1,000 THB
- **Support:** Workload Reduction ≥ 60%, Satisfaction ≥ 4.0/5.0
- **Operations:** Uptime ≥ 99.5%, Quality ≥ 90, Compliance 100%
- **Universal:** Intent Coverage ≥ 90%, Forbidden Rate ≤ 0%

---

### 7. Ownership (Article VII)

**Organizational Ownership:**
- Board: Ultimate ownership
- Executive Team: Operational ownership
- AI Governance Board: Day-to-day governance
- AI Operations Team: System ownership

**Individual Ownership:**
- CTO: Technical architecture
- Product Manager: Product strategy
- Legal & Compliance: Compliance
- Customer Service Manager: Customer experience
- Data Protection Officer: Privacy

**Accountability:** Clear ownership, consequences for violations

---

### 8. Risk Management (Article VIII)

**Risk Framework:**
- Safety, Privacy, Legal, Quality, Financial, Reputational risks
- Regular assessment and mitigation
- Monitoring and reporting

**Kill & Exit Criteria:**
- Kill: Critical violations, breaches, failures
- Exit: Financial/quality below threshold, risk failures
- See: `AI_EXIT_KILL_CRITERIA.md`

---

### 9. Compliance and Enforcement (Article IX)

**Compliance Requirements:**
- Legal: PDPA, industry regulations
- Ethical: This Charter, governance framework
- Operational: Policies, safety gates, quality standards

**Enforcement:**
- Real-time monitoring
- Automated detection
- Immediate action for violations

---

### 10. Amendments and Updates (Article X)

**Amendment Process:**
- Minor: AI Governance Board
- Major: Executive Team
- Charter: Board approval required

**Review Schedule:**
- Annual comprehensive review
- As needed for regulatory changes, incidents, strategic shifts

---
