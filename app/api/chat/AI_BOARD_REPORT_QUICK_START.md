# AI Board Report - Quick Start

**Quick reference for board-level AI report presentation.**

---

## Slide Structure (15 Slides)

1. **Title Slide**
2. **Executive Summary**
3. **Business Impact - Revenue & Growth**
4. **Business Impact - Operational Efficiency**
5. **Business Impact - Strategic Metrics**
6. **Risk Management - Overview**
7. **Risk Management - Top Critical Risks**
8. **Risk Management - Incidents & Response**
9. **Budget & ROI - Financial Performance**
10. **Budget & ROI - Value Delivered**
11. **Governance & Compliance - Framework**
12. **Governance & Compliance - Oversight**
13. **Strategic Outlook - Next Quarter**
14. **Strategic Outlook - Long-Term Vision**
15. **Q&A & Appendix**
