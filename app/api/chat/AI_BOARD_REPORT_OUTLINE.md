## Presentation Guidelines

### Timing

- **Total Duration:** 15-20 minutes
- **Per Slide:** 1-2 minutes average
- **Q&A:** 5-10 minutes
- **Total:** 20-30 minutes

### Key Principles

1. **Executive Focus:** High-level, strategic, business-oriented
2. **Data-Driven:** Metrics, trends, comparisons
3. **Visual:** Charts, graphs, dashboards
4. **Concise:** Key points only, details in appendix
5. **Actionable:** Clear recommendations and next steps

### Design Guidelines

- **Color Scheme:** Professional, company brand colors
- **Fonts:** Clear, readable, consistent
- **Charts:** Simple, labeled, easy to understand
- **Icons:** Consistent, meaningful
- **Layout:** Clean, uncluttered, balanced

### Delivery Tips

1. **Start Strong:** Executive summary sets the tone
2. **Tell a Story:** Connect metrics to business outcomes
3. **Be Transparent:** Address challenges and risks honestly
4. **Focus on Value:** Emphasize ROI and business impact
5. **End with Vision:** Inspire with strategic outlook
