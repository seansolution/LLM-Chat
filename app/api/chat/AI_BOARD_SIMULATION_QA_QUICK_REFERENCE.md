## Revenue Questions

**Q: ROI seems too good to be true?**
- Conservative assumptions (15% conversion, 25K deal value)
- Payback < 1 month reduces risk
- Kill criteria if ROI < 200% for 2Q

**Q: What if conversion rates are lower?**
- Break-even: 0.59% conversion (extremely low)
- Exit criteria: ROI < 200% for 2Q
- Even at 50% of projection, ROI > 1,000%

**Q: How do we attribute revenue?**
- AI first touchpoint only
- Track conversation → contact → conversion
- Conservative attribution approach

**Q: Will this become a cost center?**
- Costs fixed and predictable (1.8M THB/year)
- Cost ratio: 3.3% of revenue
- Exit criteria if costs exceed value

## Risk Questions

**Q: What if AI gives bad advice?**
- System prompts forbid legal/tax advice
- Real-time detection and immediate handoff
- Kill criteria: > 0% forbidden rate → Immediate shutdown

**Q: What about data breaches?**
- Encryption, access controls, monitoring
- Kill criteria: ≥ 1 breach → Immediate shutdown (< 15 min)
- 0 breaches in testing, security review completed

**Q: What if system fails?**
- Redundancy, automated failover to human
- Kill criteria: < 90% uptime for 24h → Immediate shutdown
- Target: ≥ 99.5% uptime

**Q: Regulatory changes?**
- Legal & Compliance oversight
- Kill criteria: Regulatory order → Immediate shutdown
- Quarterly compliance reviews
