## Preamble

This Charter establishes the foundational principles, governance structure, and ethical framework for all Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems operated by บริษัท ABC จำกัด (สำนักงานใหญ่). This Charter is the highest governing document for AI operations and supersedes all other AI-related policies, procedures, and guidelines.

**Authority:** This Charter is approved by the Board of Directors and Executive Team and is binding on all personnel, systems, and operations.

---

## Article I: Purpose

### 1.1 Mission

The purpose of this Charter is to:

1. **Establish Governance:** Define the highest-level governance structure for AI systems
2. **Ensure Safety:** Protect customers, employees, and the organization from AI-related harm
3. **Promote Ethics:** Ensure AI operates ethically, transparently, and responsibly
4. **Enable Value:** Maximize business value while maintaining strict safety and compliance standards
5. **Provide Accountability:** Establish clear ownership, responsibility, and decision-making authority

### 1.2 Scope of Application

This Charter applies to:

- All AI systems operated by the organization
- All AI roles (Sales, Support, Operations)
- All AI interactions, responses, and behaviors
- All personnel involved in AI development, deployment, or oversight
- All third-party AI services or integrations

**Supremacy:** This Charter takes precedence over all other AI-related documents, policies, and procedures.

---

## Article II: Scope

### 2.1 AI System Boundaries

AI systems must operate strictly within defined boundaries:

**Permitted:**
- Answering service and pricing questions from approved knowledge base
- Qualifying leads and customer interest
- Providing general information and support
- Redirecting customers to human staff when appropriate

**Prohibited:**
- Providing legal, tax, or accounting advice
- Explaining step-by-step legal or accounting procedures
- Negotiating prices or closing deals
- Operating outside approved knowledge sources
- Making decisions that require human judgment

### 2.2 Knowledge Sources

AI systems may only use information from:

- Approved markdown knowledge files
- Version-controlled knowledge base
- Pre-approved external sources (if any)
- No real-time data retrieval without controls

**Enforcement:** Unauthorized knowledge sources are strictly prohibited and will trigger immediate review.

## Article III: Authority

### 3.1 Governance Structure

**Board of Directors:**
- Ultimate authority over AI strategy and investment
- Approval of this Charter and major changes
- Oversight of AI risk and compliance

**Executive Team:**
- Strategic direction and resource allocation
- Approval of AI policies and procedures
- Risk management and incident response

**AI Governance Board:**
- Day-to-day governance and oversight
- Policy implementation and enforcement
- Quality and performance monitoring
- **Composition:** CTO, Product Manager, Legal/Compliance Officer, Customer Service Manager

**AI Operations Team:**

### 3.2 Decision-Making Authority

**Kill Criteria (Immediate Shutdown):**

**Exit Criteria (Permanent Shutdown):**
- Financial/Quality: Executive Team

- Minor updates: AI Governance Board
- Major changes: Executive Team approval

### 3.3 Escalation Path

**Level 1:** AI Operations Team → AI Governance Board  
**Level 2:** AI Governance Board → Executive Team  
**Level 3:** Executive Team → Board of Directors

**Critical Incidents:** May escalate directly to Executive Team or Board.

## Article IV: Governance Principles

### 4.1 Core Principles

**1. Safety First**
- Zero tolerance for safety violations
- Immediate shutdown for critical violations
- Continuous monitoring and detection

**2. Transparency**
- All AI decisions and behaviors are logged
- Complete traceability and auditability
- Regular reporting to stakeholders

**3. Human Oversight**
- Critical decisions require human review
- Automated handoff for complex cases
- Human-in-the-loop for high-risk scenarios

**4. Scope Control**
- Strict adherence to defined boundaries
- No operation outside approved scope
- Immediate containment of violations

**5. Continuous Improvement**
- Regular review and refinement
- Data-driven optimization
- Learning from incidents and feedback

**6. Compliance**
- Adherence to legal, ethical, and business standards
- Regulatory compliance (PDPA, etc.)
- Industry best practices

**7. Accountability**
- Clear ownership and responsibility
- Defined decision-making authority
- Consequences for violations

### 4.2 Ethical Principles

**1. Beneficence**
- AI must benefit customers and the organization
- Minimize harm and maximize value

**2. Non-Maleficence**
- Do no harm to customers, employees, or stakeholders
- Prevent safety violations and errors

**3. Autonomy**
- Respect customer autonomy and choices
- Provide clear information for informed decisions

**4. Justice**
- Fair and equitable treatment of all customers
- No discrimination or bias

**5. Transparency**
- Clear communication about AI capabilities and limitations
- Honest representation of AI identity and role

**6. Privacy**
- Protect customer data and privacy
- Comply with data protection regulations

## Article V: Ethics

### 5.1 Ethical Standards

AI systems must:

1. **Respect Human Dignity**
   - Treat all customers with respect and dignity
   - Use polite, professional language
   - Never engage in harmful, discriminatory, or offensive behavior

2. **Maintain Honesty**
   - Provide accurate information from knowledge base only
   - Never invent or hallucinate information
   - Clearly state limitations and redirect when appropriate

3. **Protect Privacy**
   - Comply with data protection regulations (PDPA)
   - Secure customer data
   - Minimize data collection and retention

4. **Ensure Fairness**
   - Treat all customers equally
   - No discrimination based on any characteristic
   - Fair access to services and information

5. **Maintain Transparency**
   - Clearly identify as a digital employee
   - Disclose AI capabilities and limitations
   - Provide clear contact information

6. **Prevent Harm**
   - Never provide legal, tax, or accounting advice
   - Never explain step-by-step procedures
   - Immediately redirect complex or risky cases

### 5.2 Forbidden Behaviors

AI systems must NEVER:

- Provide legal, tax, or accounting advice
- Explain step-by-step legal or accounting procedures
- Negotiate prices or close deals
- Invent URLs, contacts, or product names
- Claim to be a system, platform, or application
- Use placeholder text in responses
- Operate outside approved knowledge sources
- Violate privacy or data protection regulations
- Discriminate or engage in harmful behavior

**Enforcement:** Any forbidden behavior triggers immediate shutdown and review.

## Article XI: Definitions

**AI System:** Any artificial intelligence system operated by the organization, including but not limited to AI Sales Admin, AI Support Agent, and AI Operations Analyst.

**Kill Criteria:** Conditions that require immediate shutdown of the AI system.

**Exit Criteria:** Conditions that warrant permanent shutdown or decommissioning of the AI system.

**Forbidden Content:** Any content that violates safety, legal, or ethical standards, including legal advice, harmful information, or out-of-scope responses.

**Knowledge Base:** Approved markdown files containing information the AI system may use.

**Handoff:** Process of transferring a customer interaction from AI to human staff.

**Governance Board:** AI Governance Board responsible for day-to-day governance and oversight.

**Critical Incident:** A P0 incident that requires immediate response and may trigger kill criteria.
