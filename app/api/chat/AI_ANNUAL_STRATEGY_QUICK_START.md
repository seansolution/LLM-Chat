# AI Annual Strategy - Quick Start

**Quick reference for AI annual strategy.**

---

## Strategic Pillars

1. **Revenue Growth** - Maximize revenue through AI-driven lead qualification
2. **Operational Efficiency** - Optimize operations to reduce costs and improve quality
3. **Risk Control & Governance** - Maintain strong risk controls and governance

---

## Annual Targets

### Revenue
- Revenue Attributed: ≥15.7M THB
- Revenue Attribution: ≥12% of total sales
- Conversion Rate: ≥30%
- ROI: ≥2,500%

### Efficiency
- AI Coverage: 70-85%
- Support Workload Reduction: ≥60%
- Quality Score: ≥88
- Cost per Conversation: ≤170 THB

### Risk & Governance
- Critical Risk Incidents: 0
- Compliance Rate: 100%
- Forbidden Response Rate: ≤0%
- Audit Pass Rate: 100%
