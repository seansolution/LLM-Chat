## Executive Summary

This annual strategy outlines the AI system's strategic direction, focusing on revenue generation, operational efficiency, and risk control. The strategy is organized around three strategic pillars with clear initiatives, success metrics, and accountability.

**Strategic Vision:**
Transform AI from a support tool to a strategic business driver, delivering significant revenue growth, operational efficiency, and competitive advantage while maintaining strong risk controls and governance.
