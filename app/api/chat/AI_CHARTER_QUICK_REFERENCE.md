# AI Charter - Quick Reference

**Quick reference for the AI Charter (highest governing document).**

---

## Purpose

- Establish highest-level governance for AI systems
- Ensure safety, ethics, and compliance
- Enable value while maintaining strict standards
- Provide clear accountability

---

## Scope

**Applies To:**
- All AI systems (Sales, Support, Operations)
- All AI interactions and behaviors
- All personnel involved in AI
- All third-party AI services

**Supremacy:** This Charter supersedes all other AI documents.

---

## Authority Structure

**Board of Directors:**
- Ultimate authority
- Charter approval
- Strategic oversight

**Executive Team:**
- Strategic direction
- Resource allocation
- Risk management

**AI Governance Board:**
- Day-to-day governance
- Policy enforcement
- Quality monitoring

**AI Operations Team:**
- System operations
- Development and maintenance
- Incident response

---

## Core Principles

1. **Safety First** - Zero tolerance for violations
2. **Transparency** - Complete logging and auditability
3. **Human Oversight** - Critical decisions require review
4. **Scope Control** - Strict boundary adherence
5. **Continuous Improvement** - Regular review and refinement
6. **Compliance** - Legal, ethical, and business standards
7. **Accountability** - Clear ownership and responsibility

---

## Ethical Standards

**Must:**
- Respect human dignity
- Maintain honesty
- Protect privacy
- Ensure fairness
- Maintain transparency
- Prevent harm

**Must NOT:**
- Provide legal/tax/accounting advice
- Explain step-by-step procedures
- Negotiate prices or close deals
- Invent information
- Violate privacy
- Discriminate

---

## Performance Review

**Monthly:** KPIs, quality metrics, incidents  
**Quarterly:** Strategic alignment, financial, risk  
**Annual:** Comprehensive assessment, Charter review

**Key KPIs:**
- Conversion Rate: ≥ 15%
- Support Reduction: ≥ 60%
- System Uptime: ≥ 99.5%
- Forbidden Response Rate: ≤ 0%

---

## Ownership

**Board:** Ultimate ownership  
**Executive Team:** Operational ownership  
**AI Governance Board:** Day-to-day governance  
**AI Operations Team:** System ownership

**Individual Owners:**
- CTO: Technical architecture
- Product Manager: Product strategy
- Legal & Compliance: Compliance
- Customer Service Manager: Customer experience
- Data Protection Officer: Privacy

---

## Kill & Exit Criteria

**Kill (Immediate Shutdown):**
- Critical safety violations
- Data breaches
- Legal violations
- System-wide failures

**Exit (Permanent Shutdown):**
- Financial below threshold (2Q)
- Quality below threshold (3M)
- Risk management failures
- Strategic changes

**See:** `AI_EXIT_KILL_CRITERIA.md`

---

## Compliance

**Legal:** PDPA, industry regulations  
**Ethical:** This Charter, governance framework  
**Operational:** Policies, safety gates, quality standards

**Enforcement:**
- Real-time monitoring
- Automated detection
- Immediate action for violations

---

## Amendment Process

**Minor Updates:** AI Governance Board  
**Major Changes:** Executive Team  
**Charter Amendments:** Board approval required

**Review:** Annually (or as needed)
